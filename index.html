Ankita Ghosh

Hello, I am Ankita Ghosh, an MSc Computer Science graduate from ETH Zürich, Switzerland, where I majored in visual and interactive computing. I am currently a Teaching Assistant for the Master of Advanced Studies in AI and Digital Technology at ETH Zurich. My interest lies in computer vision, particularly its applications in interdisciplinary domains like healthcare AI.

I have worked with the ARCADE lab led by Prof. Mathias Unberath at Johns Hopkins University, the Computer Vision and Learning Group led by Prof. Dr. Siyu Tang at ETH Zurich, the Social and Intelligent Robotics Research Laboratory at the University of Waterloo (Mitacs Globalink), and the Spectrum Lab at the Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore. I also interned at Stryker as a Machine Vision Engineer in Freiburg, Germany. My work spans applied AI across healthcare, social robotics, model interpretability, and 3D modeling and manipulation for digital humans and robots.

I am always open to interdisciplinary research, so if you would like to discuss my projects or a potential collaboration, feel free to email me.
